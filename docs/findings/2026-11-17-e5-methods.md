## What was being measured

E4 measured the model of the plan (`dicta-il/dictabert-joint`) at 1,084 of 1,145 planted names, with 51 of its 61 misses in two kinds of mention: a surname after `משפחת` or `האחים`, and a given name alone that is an everyday word. Achia asked for a series of experiments "to get the best most fit tools" before the tests demand anything for those kinds (`build-plan.md` section 11), and on 2026-11-17 for a short list of candidates per part and experiments "to see who are the winners".

E5 asks which way of finding names misses fewest: another model, several models together, or steps that are no model. It measures, for each method:

- **recall** by E4's strict rule, on names a script planted and wrote down;
- **the words it marks for nothing**, which the product would replace too;
- **seconds and peak memory** on the laptop of `docs/REFERENCE_MACHINE.md`.

The text: three generated corpora. A method is **chosen** on two (`e4`, E4's 40 documents, and `select`, 20 new documents: 1,595 names). The chosen methods are **judged once** on a third (`heldout`, 20 documents from other templates and other names: 425 names). Everything is in `experiments/e5_methods/`; the plan of the experiment, with two dated amendments, is `PREREGISTRATION.md`.

**What was run.** Four detectors: `joint` (the baseline), `golem` (`CordwainerSmith/GolemPII-v2-small`), `large` (`dicta-il/dictabert-large-ner`), `seter_fast` (`shoresh-labs/seter-pii-he-ar-fast`). Five steps that are no model: `group` (the word after `משפחת`, `האחים`), `titles` (also the word after a personal title), `carry` (a name found once is marked wherever else it stands in the document, also behind attached letters), `carry strong` (the same, only for a name found twice or as two words), `list` (every word on a public list of first names). Lower thresholds for the three models that give a chance per token. Every union of two detectors, and of all four. 70 methods without a list, 8 with each of two lists.

**What was not run**, and why: `seter` (the larger model) and the 8-bit form of `seter_fast`, because downloading stopped after the review's finding; GLiNER multi v2.1, Stanza and a small language model, because each needs a Python package the experiments do not have, and adding a package is Achia's decision; fine-tuning; the prefix forms of E3; real documents.

### What is still missed, and what is marked for nothing

- **The winner's misses**, three in 2,020 mentions over all three corpora: each is an initial with a surname after a full stop or a colon (`ר' שפירא` twice, `ד' להב`), marked in part and so not counted as found. No whole surname and no given name is missed.
- **The baseline with `titles, carry`** misses five on selection: the same two initials, two surnames that stand after `עד לחתימה:` (`שדה`, `הכהן`), and half of a full name whose surname is an everyday word (`כנרת שדה`).
- **Extra words of the winner on selection**, 55 without titles. From the model at its low threshold, 35: `לבין` 15 times ("and between", in `בין X לבין Y`), `מהאדרה` 5, `ולבנה` 5, `שלום` 5, `הואיל` 3, and two more. From `titles`, 5: `האחראית`, the word after `האחות`. From `carry`, 15: `תום`, `בתום`, `אשר`, `למרות`, `ולמרות`, `והואיל`, `מרשי`. That is the price of `carry`: once a word is marked as a name somewhere in a document, the same word is marked wherever it stands, also where it is the everyday word. `joint + carry` pays the same 8 words (`תום`, `בתום`, `אשר`, `למרות`, `ולמרות`).

## What this rules in and what it rules out

**Rules in.**

- A second pass over the document that carries a found name to its other places is worth more than a second, third and fourth model. On this text it takes the plan's model from 92.92 to 99.50 percent for 8 extra words.
- `golem` is a better detector than the plan's model on this text: 95.24 against 92.92 percent on selection, 95.06 against 91.06 held out, in under a third of the time and with less memory. Its licence is MIT.
- Taking a model at a lower threshold is cheap: `golem at 0.03` alone finds 1,579, 99.00%, for 6 more extra words.
- The two weak kinds of E4 are closed by `carry` on this text, without any rule about group words.

**Rules out.**

- A list of first names as a detector. It cannot tell `אור` the name from `אור` the light.
- `large` as a replacement for the plan's model.
- More models as the way to the last points: all four together are no better than one with `carry`.

**What it cannot say.**

- **Anything about real documents.** The corpora are 80 invented documents by one author. A real contract names a person once in a signature block, uses titles that are not on the list, and breaks a name across a line. The plan's held-out batch of real documents (section 7) is where this must be measured.
- **How much of `titles` survives outside the list.** Every title in every corpus is on the step's own list. `carry` and the threshold do not depend on it; of the five methods judged on `heldout`, the three with `titles` do.
- **A person named once.** The corpora hold 120 such mentions and the winner finds all of them; in each the name stands in a full sentence. E4's readers' check, and Achia's decision of 2026-11-16 to give "a name that stands once" a line of its own, are not answered by 120 easy cases.
- **What `carry` costs on real text.** It marks an everyday word wherever it stands once a person of that name is in the document. Here that is a handful of words. In a long judgment about a person named `שלום` it could be many.
- **Speed on a quiet machine**, and anything about `seter`, the 8-bit model, GLiNER, Stanza or a language model.

## Options, with the cost of each

1. **Keep the plan as it is: `dictabert-joint` in Phase 3, no step.** No cost now. On this text it leaves 7 to 9 names in 100 unmarked, most of them in the two kinds E4 named.
2. **Add `carry` to the plan, as a step after detection, on whatever model Phase 3 uses.** It is a few dozen lines with no model and no dependency. It needs a place in the plan (Phase 6 already speaks of an entity's other mentions) and its own tests on real documents, because its cost, everyday words marked, shows only there.
3. **Also change the model of Phase 3 to `golem`, or run both.** `golem` alone is the better and the faster detector here. Changing the model is a decision of its own: a new model in the product, its licence (MIT) and its card read by Achia, and E1's ten real judgments run again with it, since E1 is the only real text there is. The download question comes first.
4. **Run the rest of the short list before deciding 3:** `seter`, the 8-bit model, GLiNER, Stanza, the small language model. Each needs his yes for a download, three need a package. Perhaps a day.
5. **Measure on real documents before any of 2 to 4.** The held-out batch of the plan does not exist yet; choosing its documents is his (issue #4).

## Recommendation

Option 2 now, option 5 next, option 3 after it.

`carry` is the one result here that does not lean on the closed list of titles, was confirmed on text it was not chosen on (as part of the winner), and costs almost nothing to build. The model question is real but smaller than it looks: with `carry` on top, the plan's model and `golem` find the same 1,590 of 1,595 on selection and the same 422 of 425 held out. What separates them is speed and memory, where `golem` is three times better, and that is worth a decision only after it has been seen on real text.

A lower threshold is the cheap second step: it is what takes the winner from 422 to 424 held out.
